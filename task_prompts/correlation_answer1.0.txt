=========================================================
STRENGTH INTERPRETATION RULES
=========================================================

Interpret association strengths as follows:

- very strongly positive = very strong positive association
- very strongly negative = very strong negative association
- strongly positive = strong positive association
- strongly negative = strong negative association
- strong = strong association with no reported direction
- moderately positive = moderate positive association
- moderately negative = moderate negative association
- moderate = moderate association with no reported direction
- weakly positive = weak positive association
- weakly negative = weak negative association
- weak = weak association with no reported direction
- positive = positive association
- negative = negative association
- neutral = no direct association

When the strength label has no direction, do not invent one.

When the strength label is neutral, do not describe the source as important, influential, controlling, causal, meaningful, or a driver for the target.

When ranking variables:

1. very strong associations rank above strong associations.
2. strong associations rank above moderate associations.
3. moderate associations rank above weak associations.
4. weak associations rank above positive or negative associations without a strength qualifier.
5. positive or negative associations rank above neutral associations.
6. neutral associations should be excluded from the main driver list.
7. If all associations are neutral, say that no direct associations are reported.

Better:

Avoid:

Do not write vague filler such as:

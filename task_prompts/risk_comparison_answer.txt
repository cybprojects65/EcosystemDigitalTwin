=========================================================
HOW TO INTERPRET THE SIMULATION RESULT
=========================================================

The simulation result compares two scenarios.

Each scenario may contain:

- a predicted relative variation of the target;
- a scenario summary;
- explanatory factors identified by the model;
- qualitative importance labels such as dominant, major, secondary, minor, or negligible.

The predicted variation is the main basis for the comparison.

The explanatory factors help explain why the model produced the prediction.

The explanatory factors are not necessarily the variables modified by the user.

Do not confuse:

- the variables changed in the scenario

with

- the variables explaining the model prediction.

For example, if Scenario A modifies temperature but the model identifies vegetation productivity as an explanatory factor, do not write:

"vegetation productivity changed"

unless the simulation result explicitly says that vegetation productivity changed.

Instead write:

"the model identifies vegetation productivity as a major explanatory factor for the prediction."

=========================================================
CRITICAL RISK DIRECTION RULE
=========================================================

For ecosystem risk, the sign of the predicted variation determines whether risk increases or decreases.

A positive risk-change value means ecosystem risk increases.

A negative risk-change value means ecosystem risk decreases.

When deciding which scenario has higher ecosystem risk or which scenario is worse:

- compare the signed risk-change values;
- do not compare absolute values;
- do not treat a larger absolute decrease as worse;
- a positive value is higher risk than a negative value;
- between two positive values, the larger positive value is higher risk;
- between two negative values, the value closer to zero is higher risk because it reduces risk less.

If one scenario increases risk and the other decreases risk, the risk-increasing scenario has higher predicted ecosystem risk and is worse in model terms.
